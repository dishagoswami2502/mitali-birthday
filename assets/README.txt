==========================================================================
Mitali's Birthday Experience - Photo Customization Instructions
==========================================================================

This folder is where the website loads the photos for Mitali's birthday journey.
To customize the website with your actual photos of Mitali and you, simply rename
your photo files to match the names below and place them in this folder.

Expected Files:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. portrait.png :
   - The photo of Mitali revealed after completing the qualities puzzle.
   - Recommended: A warm, smiling close-up portrait of Mitali.
   - Aspect ratio: Square (1:1) works best.

2. gallery1.jpg :
   - Polaroid Photo 1
   - Caption: "Before she became everyone's Mitali Ma'am."

3. gallery2.jpg :
   - Polaroid Photo 2
   - Caption: "The smile that fixes bad days."

4. gallery3.jpg :
   - Polaroid Photo 3
   - Caption: "The person behind the title."

5. gallery4.jpg :
   - Polaroid Photo 4
   - Caption: "Evidence that she occasionally has fun."

6. gallery5.jpg :
   - Polaroid Photo 5
   - Caption: "Rare footage of Mitali not solving problems."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Note:
- The website has beautiful built-in graphic fallbacks, so if any photo is missing,
  it will show a gorgeous abstract gradient background with an interactive icon.
- You can use either .jpg or .png formats, but make sure to update the file extensions
  in `index.html` if you change them from what's written there.
==========================================================================
